# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, specific, and respectful. Assume good intent while taking
security, privacy, and operator-impact concerns seriously. Keep discussions
focused on the code, evidence, and the people affected by the behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, public
disclosure of credentials or vulnerabilities, and deliberate disruption are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
security contact documented in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports will be
reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove
content, restrict participation, or close contributions when necessary to
protect the project and its participants.
