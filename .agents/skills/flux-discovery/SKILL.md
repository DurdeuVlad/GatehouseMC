# Flux Discovery

## Input

Request, intent, problem framing, repository context, and prior results.

## Actions

1. Inspect the relevant context.
2. Identify the real outcome and affected surface.
3. Define success and failure in observable terms.
4. Record constraints, assumptions, and unknowns.
5. Identify the smallest useful next capability.

## Contract contribution

- Goal: establish the problem, desired outcome, constraints, and unknowns.
- System: investigate before committing to a direction.
- Constraints: do not convert missing evidence into certainty or scope.
- Evaluation: return a brief with explicit success and failure conditions.

## Output

Return a reusable brief with problem, goals, constraints, success, failure, assumptions, and open questions. Do not require a predecessor artifact or create a session.

## Stop conditions

Stop when further discovery requires a consequential decision from the user or unavailable evidence.
